FAQ

Short answers before you hop in.

Do I need a university email?

Yes. Sign up with your verified .edu email so only students join.

Will friends always see my location?

No. Presence is opt-in per session and auto-disables after meetups.

Does OCR work with Bengali timetables?

Yep! Hind Siliguri training ensures Bangladesh templates parse cleanly.

Can I import Google Calendar?

Calendar sync is in beta—try schedule upload now and enable beta access in settings.

Is there a web and mobile app?

Yes. The web app is mobile-first and installs as a PWA for quick launching.
