FAQ

Short answers before you hop in.

Who can sign up right now?

Students with a verified @iub.edu.bd email. We’re working to add more universities next.

Can I register with Gmail or a friend’s email?

No. University email verification is required so every profile maps to a real campus identity.

Does the app cost anything?

No. It’s free for students.

How do I install it on my phone?

Open the web app in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS) and tap Add to Home Screen. It behaves like an app after install.

How do I add my schedule?

Paste your IRAS schedule into Profile → Schedule Import, to detect your courses in seconds.

Can I fix mistakes or add custom slots?

Yes. Tap any slot to edit, or use Add row to create custom time ranges.

Who can see my schedule or campus presence?

Only friends you’ve added and mutually granted visibility to. You can hide both anytime with one tap.

Do I have to share GPS for campus presence?

No. Being connected to campus Wi-Fi can set your status to On campus. GPS is only used as a fallback, and we don’t sell or share your data.

Are you here to make a profit off students?

Hiya, dev here. No, this application is student-owned, community-serving and you make it whole. I am a student just like you. I go to IUB, and I planned this application since my second semester and worked hundreds of hours on it to fill gaps I personally faced. I met some of the best teachers and friends in my life in IUB, so this is my thank you to the community that has given me so much.
