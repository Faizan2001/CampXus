About MeetchuThere

A modern schedule management app for university students, built with love and lots of learning!

About This App

MeetchuThere is a schedule comparison and management tool designed specifically for university students. It helps you:

  • Manage your weekly class schedule
  • Connect with classmates and friends
  • Compare schedules to find study times
  • Access your schedule on any device

About the Developer

University Student & Aspiring Developer
