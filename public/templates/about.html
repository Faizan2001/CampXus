Hey! I’m Faizan, a 4th-year CSC student at IUB. I built MeetchuThere because my friends and I were tired of juggling screenshots and messy group chats just to figure out who’s free. Now, you can instantly see who’s on campus and when your friends are free— no chaos, no guesswork.

What it helps you do

  • Import your class routine in seconds.
  • Spot overlapping free time with friends instantly.
  • Share when you're on campus with your friends.
  • Give you complete privacy control of your profile.
